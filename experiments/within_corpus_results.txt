FINDINGS:
1. Within-corpus success is UNIVERSAL (emb-within 0.74-0.97) — every dim decodable inside its corpora
   by a probe that never fine-tuned. Within-corpus AUROC is deceptive.
2. That within signal is LARGELY LEXICAL: bow-within ~ emb-within everywhere; for RIGHTS bow-within
   (0.78) is HIGHER than emb (0.74). So "decodable within corpus" does NOT establish a coherent
   concept -- only that labels are not random. (Retracts the earlier "coherent, not incoherent" claim.)
3. Frozen emb-cross is at chance for MOST dims (env 0.14, epis 0.22, fair 0.25, care 0.33, legit 0.39,
   rights 0.45) -- the raw space does not align disparate corpora; that is what fine-tuning is FOR.
   Fine-tuning rescues cross-transfer for all 8 other dims (-> 0.62-0.85) but NOT rights (0.45->0.475).
   RIGHTS is the ONLY dimension whose fine-tuned cross-transfer stays at chance.
VERDICT: rights is UNTRANSFERABLE, not unlearnable -- within signal present but corpus/jurisdiction-
specific surface that fine-tuning cannot align. Consistent with framework-relativity. Lean on "only
dim fine-tuning cannot lift above chance", NOT "largest drop" (the frozen-drop is a continuum;
environmental drops most).
