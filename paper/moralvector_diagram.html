Two projections, two different measurement properties

The instrument reads each cell along two independent projections, drawn in the paper's two accent colors:

Valence — the cell's sign. Register-invariant: it transfers across corpora with or without adversarial de-confounding.

Presence / identity — the cell's magnitude and which foundation is engaged. For several dimensions it is register-bound — it lives in the same subspace as corpus/register and cannot be pried apart from it.

03 The headline relationship

Across three independent probes, moral valence and moral identity dissociate: a dimension's valence transfers across register; its identity, for several foundations, does not.

That is why the matrix above is two-colored. The blue channels collapse onto a single general factor G — you can read whether a norm is upheld, but not cleanly separate which foundation is engaged from the register it was written in. Cross-dataset transfer, not in-domain accuracy, is the minimal evidence that any of these channels measures a construct at all.
