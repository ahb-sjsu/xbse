The two new pieces — the three-probe decomposition and the confirmed pre-registration — are both genuinely well executed, and §3.5 in particular is the strongest section of the paper now: you ran the BoW-within control I asked for, it torpedoed your own "coherent, not incoherent" claim, and you retracted it *visibly in the text* while finding the sharper true statement underneath ("the only dimension fine-tuning cannot lift above chance" is better evidence for your thesis than the retracted version was). The frozen-cross column also quietly reframes what fine-tuning *is* in this setup — the raw space doesn't align corpora for anyone; InfoNCE is the alignment mechanism; rights is where it fails — which is a cleaner mechanistic story than the paper had before. That said, this is the round where the confirmation arrived, so it's the round to be most suspicious. Three substantive issues.

**1. §3.7 proves less than §4 now claims — by the paper's own logic.** The upgrade to "necessary AND sufficient… now shown causally" and the abstract's "clinches the direction of cause" conflate two claims of very different strength. What §3.7 cleanly establishes, with the internal control doing real work: *the collapse is caused by corpus-sharing and is removable by a data intervention*. That direction is solid — legit↔epis kept shared, stayed collapsed; same run, only the corpus changed. But the converse — that independence produced *concept*-distinct care and fairness encoders — is exactly the claim your own §3.3/§6 symmetric-confound discipline forbids. Re-corpusing care changed two things at once: the training signal *and the register of its evaluation pairs*. `care→epistemic` falling from 0.871 to 0.42 is equally consistent with "care_v2 learned care-the-concept" and with "care_v2 learned Moral-Stories/MHS register and can no longer read Social-Chem↔ETHICS pairs" — the identical register-shift explanation you carefully preserve for privacy's off-diagonals. So the table's verdicts should read "decoupled (corpus-level)," not "distinct"; §4 should say corpus independence is necessary and sufficient *for corpus-level specificity*, with concept-level sufficiency still gated on the register-controlled test (your own open question 2, which §3.7 does not discharge); and "clinches the direction of cause" should become "shows the collapse is removable." The asymmetry is worth stating explicitly, because half of it *is* now causal: shared corpus → collapse is proven by intervention-plus-control; independence → distinct concepts is not yet.

**2. care_v2 and fair_v2 still share a corpus — and the matrix shows it.** Both kept Social-Chem; only ETHICS was replaced. Their mutual cross-transfer, 0.68 symmetric (care_v2→fair_v2 = 0.701), is by far the largest off-diagonal in the new 4×4 — sitting well above chance and right where the residual shared corpus sits, while their transfers to legit/epis fell to 0.42–0.56. The text's "decouple from everything" glides past this, and the care_v2↔fair_v2 "distinct" verdict (+0.16, the smallest gap) is precisely the pair with the residual share — two independent reasons for caution converging on the same cell. But there's a gift here if you frame it honestly: you now have a rough dose-response — share both corpora ≈ 0.74–0.87 cross, share one ≈ 0.68, share none ≈ 0.42–0.53 (it's not perfectly monotone; care_v2↔legit also share Social-Chem and sit at 0.52, so quantity-of-shared-*pairs* likely matters, not just corpus count — worth a sentence). A gradient in cross-transfer that tracks degree of corpus overlap is a stronger and more honest headline than "decoupled from everything," and the obvious follow-up (drop Social-Chem from one of the two, predict the 0.68 falls) practically pre-registers itself.

**3. Two matrix anomalies remain unexplained, one of them for the second time.** First, `rights→privacy = 0.66` — I flagged the likely cause last round (ECHR Article 8 *is* the right to private life, so your rights corpus contains privacy cases) and it's still the largest anomaly in the 9×9 with no comment; the Art-8-exclusion check is an afternoon and either result is publishable. Second, new in §3.5: environmental's frozen emb-cross of **0.14** is not "no transfer," it's strong *inverted* transfer — the raw-space probe systematically predicts the *wrong* label across corpora, meaning some surface feature is anticorrelated with the label between ClimateBERT and the dual-judged claims (plausible: green/climate vocabulary marks positives in one corpus and greenwashing-negatives in the other). That fine-tuning takes an inverted alignment (0.14) to 0.817 is remarkable and currently unremarked; a reviewer will circle that cell, and it's also your most vivid illustration of why raw-space lexical alignment is untrustworthy — one sentence turns it from anomaly into supporting evidence.
